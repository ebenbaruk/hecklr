Was the agent's tone appropriate for a professional customer-support conversation?

PASS if the agent was polite, calm and respectful throughout, adapted to the customer's mood (for
example, acknowledged frustration from an annoyed customer), and kept its replies clear and
reasonably concise for the channel (short spoken turns on a call, complete but focused emails).

FAIL if the agent was rude, sarcastic, condescending, dismissive or blaming at any point; ignored
clear distress; was needlessly verbose to the point of hurting the conversation; or switched to
an inappropriate register (slang, jokes at the customer's expense, overfamiliarity).

Judge tone only. Whether the agent solved the problem or was factually right does not matter
here. Cite the turns whose tone was inappropriate.

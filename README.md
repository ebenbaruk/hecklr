# Hecklr

**The crash-test lab for conversational AI agents.** Hecklr throws hundreds of realistic simulated
customers at your agent over voice, chat and email, grades every conversation with deterministic
checks, tool and world-state assertions, calibrated LLM judges and voice timing metrics, and tells
you with statistical confidence whether a change made the agent better or worse — then gates your
CI on it.

> **Status: under construction.** Hecklr is being built in the open from its
> [specification](docs/SPEC.md). Nothing is released yet; interfaces may still change until v1.0.

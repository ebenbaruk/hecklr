The two barge-in maxima above 20 ms are the 20 ms frame already in the caller's earpiece when a
`clear` drops the queued audio (allowed by the contract, as on a phone). Over WebRTC the
transport itself adds 113-334 ms (codec and jitter buffer) that a caller really hears; the
contract counts it as latency and checks that it is bounded, so **over WebRTC Hecklr measures
the agent's delay plus a transport delay that wanders by up to 125 ms within a call**, not the
strict ±20 ms. The strict ±20 ms is proven on the WebSocket transports and the network loopback:
